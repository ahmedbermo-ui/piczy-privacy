Piczy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Piczy itself does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. The ad banner (see Ads) is shown by Google, which collects some data to do so.

What stays on your device

  • Your puzzles, progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • If you choose one of your own photos, the app keeps a copy inside its private storage so the puzzle can be restored. The photo is never uploaded.
  • The app uses the system photo picker, so it does not need access to your photo library.
  • If your phone backs up to your Google account (an Android setting you control), Android may include Piczy's saved puzzles in that backup. The developer has no access to it.

Downloads

The app downloads the list of photo packs, small preview pictures, and pack photos from ahmedbermo-ui.github.io (GitHub Pages). These are plain file downloads: nothing about you or your puzzles is sent. Like any website, GitHub may briefly log the network address of the request; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Gift puzzles

If you send a gift puzzle, the app makes one file with your photo, the board size and your optional message, and hands it to the app you choose in the share menu (for example WhatsApp). It goes only where you send it; Piczy has no server. The message is hidden until the puzzle is solved, but it is a surprise, not encryption: anyone with the file could read it. When you open a gift file, Piczy reads it on your phone and keeps the photo and message there.

Ads

Players without Piczy Plus see one small banner on the puzzle screen, shown by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location from your network address, and how you interact with the ad. In the EEA and UK the app asks for your consent first; you can change it any time in Settings › About › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. Ads never get your photos, puzzles or messages. See how Google uses data for ads. Piczy Plus removes the ads.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google, and the app only learns which products you own, so it can unlock them.

What the app does not do

  • It has no analytics code of its own, and the developer gets no data about you.
  • It never uploads your photos or puzzles.
  • It does not know who you are.

Sharing

If you tap Share on the finish screen, the picture and a short message are handed to the app you choose in your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick a destination.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved. You can also delete a single puzzle from the In progress list on Home, or in Solved.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.

Contact

Use the developer contact details shown on the Piczy page in Google Play.
