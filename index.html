Piczy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Piczy does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Your puzzles, progress, streak and settings are saved only on your device.
  • If you choose one of your own photos, the app keeps a copy inside its private storage so the puzzle can be restored. The photo is never uploaded.
  • The app uses the system photo picker, so it does not need access to your photo library.
  • If your phone backs up to your Google account (an Android setting you control), Android may include Piczy's saved puzzles in that backup. The developer has no access to it.

Downloads

The app downloads the list of photo packs, small preview pictures, and the photos of packs you buy from ahmedbermo-ui.github.io (GitHub Pages). These are plain file downloads: nothing about you or your puzzles is sent. Like any website, GitHub may briefly log the network address of the request; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google, and the app only learns which products you own, so it can unlock them.

What the app does not do

  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking code.
  • It never uploads your photos or puzzles.
  • It does not know who you are.

Sharing

If you tap Share on the finish screen, the picture and a short message are handed to the app you choose in your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick a destination.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved. You can also delete a single puzzle from the Gallery with a long press.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.

Contact

Use the developer contact details shown on the Piczy page in Google Play.
